Your day stays yours.

Privacy · last changed 22 November 2026

KRNL0 is an app on your computer. There is no KRNL0 account, no tracking and no ads. Nothing about you or your plans is sent to the people who make KRNL0.

What KRNL0 keeps, and where

Your board (plans, events, habits, notes, recorded time) is a file on your computer. If you run your own KRNL0 server, your computers and phone share that board through it, and copies are kept on that server. Both are yours: we never see them.

Google and Apple calendars

You can sign in to Google or iCloud in Calendar › Add your calendars. KRNL0 talks to Google or Apple directly from your computer. There is no server of ours in between.

  • What it asks for. Google: access to see and edit your calendars, and your email address (to show which account is signed in). iCloud: your Apple ID email and an app-specific password you make at account.apple.com, never your Apple ID password.
  • What it reads. The calendars you tick, so their events show on your KRNL0 Calendar.
  • What it writes. Only if you switch on "Put KRNL0 on your … too" when you sign in: one calendar named "KRNL0 · your board", kept the same as your board both ways (your events and your plans with a time). KRNL0 never changes or deletes anything in your other calendars.
  • Where it is kept. Your sign-in stays on your computer, locked by your system's own keychain. It is never written into your board and never sent anywhere else. Events read from your calendars are saved in your board, so they are on your own server too if you use one.
  • How to stop. Sign out in Settings › Calendars. You can also take KRNL0's access away at any time in your Google account (Security › Third-party access) or by deleting the app-specific password at account.apple.com.

Google user data

KRNL0's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Your Google calendar data is used only to show your calendars in KRNL0 and keep the KRNL0 calendar in step.
  • It is not sold, not used for ads, and not used to train AI models.
  • Nobody reads it. It never reaches the people who make KRNL0.
  • If you ask KRNL0's assistant to look at your board and you chose an online model for it, what it reads (which can include calendar events on your board) goes to the model provider you chose, to answer you. This happens only when you use the assistant.

When KRNL0 goes online

Only for things you set up: calendars you sign in to, your own KRNL0 server, an assistant model you chose, and checking GitHub for a new version of KRNL0.

Questions

KRNL0 is open source: you can read exactly what it does on GitHub. Ask anything there in an issue. If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
