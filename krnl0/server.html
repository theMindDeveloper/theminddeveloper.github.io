What you need

KRNL0 works the same without a server. The server only adds your other devices.

  • A computer that stays onA Raspberry Pi, a NAS, a home server or an old laptop. 64-bit Linux, or anything that runs Docker.
  • KRNL0 0.13 or newerOn each computer you want in sync. On Windows the app updates itself; on Mac and Linux it tells you when a new version is out.
  • About ten minutesMost of it is the download. You'll choose one password on the way.
1

Install the server

Pick one. Both give you the same server.

With Docker

A NAS, a home server, or Docker Desktop on Windows and Mac.

Linux and Mac · terminal 
$ curl -fsSL https://github.com/theMindDeveloper/krnl0-server/releases/latest/download/docker.sh | sh
Windows · PowerShell 
> irm https://github.com/theMindDeveloper/krnl0-server/releases/latest/download/docker.ps1 | iex
  1. Paste it and press Enter.It finds your address and time zone, sets up KRNL0 and its updater in krnl0 in your home folder, and starts them.
  2. Answer one question."Update by itself at night?" Either way, updates are one click in the KRNL0 app. At the end it shows the address and a one-time setup code for step 2.

Already ran KRNL0 with docker run? The same command moves it over and keeps your data. Prefer to do it by hand? compose.yml and env.example (save it as .env), then docker volume create krnl0-data and docker compose up -d.

On Linux, one command

Raspberry Pi OS, Ubuntu, Debian and most others, 64-bit.

terminal 
$ curl -fsSL https://github.com/theMindDeveloper/krnl0-server/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
  1. Paste it into the server's terminal.It checks the download, then installs KRNL0 as a locked-down service with its own user, and its updater.
  2. Answer a few questions.Right in the terminal: what the server does, encryption, its address, your password, and whether it may update itself at night. Step 2 shows them.

Curious what it runs? Read install.sh first; it's short. After that, updates are one click in the KRNL0 app.

2

Make it yours

One owner, with a password only you know.

On Linux, the installer asks you in the terminal and ends with a QR code for your phone. With Docker, you do the same in your browser, with the setup code it printed.

  1. Open the address in a browser.The one it printed, like https://192.168.1.20:8443. On Windows it opens by itself.
  2. Continue past the warning, once.The server makes its own certificate, so the browser asks first: choose Advanced, then Continue. Want no warning at all? Use your own domain or Tailscale.
  3. Type the setup code, then choose a password.Twelve characters or more. It's your login on every device from now on.
  4. That's the server done.It makes a backup every day by itself, and before every new version.
3

Open it on your phone

No app to install. KRNL0 opens in its browser.

Today puts the timer, your plans and your habits on one screen. Calendar, Habits, Insights and the Canvas are one tap away, and Focus shows the timer full size while it runs.

  1. Scan the QR code, or type the address.The same one as on the computer. Your phone needs to be on your home Wi-Fi, or anywhere with Tailscale or your own domain.
  2. Continue past the warning, once.Your phone asks too. On an iPhone tap Show Details, then visit this website.
  3. Log in with your password.Your board is there, with everything your computers did.
  4. Hold a finger on a plan or a habit.It opens the same menu as a right-click. On the Canvas, one finger moves and two zoom.
4

Connect your computers

Once on each computer with the KRNL0 app.

Each computer keeps working offline and catches up when it's back. Edits made on two devices at the same moment are both kept.

  1. Click saved in the top bar.Then click Connect server.
  2. Type the server's address, then Find.KRNL0 shows the server's fingerprint: eight groups of letters and numbers.
  3. Check that it matches.On the server, krnl0 fingerprint shows the same groups (with Docker: docker exec krnl0 krnl0 fingerprint). Then tick It matches my server.
  4. Type your password, then Connect.The badge now says saved · synced. Do the same on your other computers.
Encrypted, alwaysEvery connection is HTTPS. The app checks the server's fingerprint, and its key stays in your computer's keychain. Passwords are stored only as hashes.
Your data, one folderEverything lives in one folder on your server, with a backup every day. Nothing is sent anywhere, apart from a daily check for a new version, which you can switch off.
Updates in one clickWhen a new version is out, the KRNL0 app says so: click Update server and type your password, or let it update itself at night. It backs up first and goes back by itself if anything fails.

Everyday commands

Type them on the server. With Docker, put docker exec -it krnl0 in front.

krnl0 status
Is it running, its address and fingerprint, and whether a new version is out.
krnl0 doctor
Checks everything, and says how to fix what isn't right.
krnl0 2fa enable
Adds a code from your phone to every login.
krnl0 devices
The computers connected to it. Remove one you no longer use.
krnl0 update
Is a new version out, and how did the last update go. krnl0 update now updates.
krnl0 backup
A backup right now, on top of the daily ones.
krnl0 help
Everything else, in plain words.
